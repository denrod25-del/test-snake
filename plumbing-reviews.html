How the grade is calculated.

Each company receives a composite score from 0–100 based on four weighted components, then mapped to a letter grade (A+ through F). The formula is deliberately simple and reproducible.

50% weight

Weighted star rating

Average rating across all platforms, weighted by review volume per platform so a single low-traffic source can't skew the result.

20% weight

Review volume

Logarithmic scale up to ~2,000 reviews. Rewards companies with substantial public track record without letting national chains dominate.

20% weight

Complaint ratio

Share of 1- and 2-star reviews plus formal BBB complaints. A company can have a high average rating and still lose points here if low-stars cluster.

10% weight

Owner response rate

Percentage of reviews the company has publicly responded to. Signals accountability and active reputation management.
