Property Intelligence signals

The Property Intelligence module combines several feeds. See data/signals/catalog.json for machine-readable status.

  • Zoning (Live) — Palm Beach County PZB ArcGIS district + recent map DT_CHG updates.
  • Ownership (Live) — County Property Appraiser GIS current owner / last sale; PBC includes deed book/page + clerk Official Records link.
  • New permits (Cached) — Tyler EnerGov scrapes for WPB, Boca, Jupiter, St. Lucie; recent slice in data/signals/recent-permits.json.
  • Rent comps (Live, Pro) — RentCast long-term rent AVM via /api/rent-lookup (shares AVM credits).
  • Insurance / flood (Live GIS + Coming Soon rates) — Live flood stamps for Palm Beach, Miami-Dade, Broward, Hillsborough, and Pinellas + FEMA MSC deep-links. Carrier premium rate-shift feeds still Coming Soon. Layer registry: data/signals/flood-layers.json.
  • Broward ownership enrich (Live) — FOLIO polygon match plus BCPA House Number layer for owner name and situs address (confirm on web.bcpa.net).
  • Tax delinquencies (Cached) — sales.json tax deed calendars + Palm Beach county-held certificates + LienHub/legal-notice portals for Hillsborough, Broward, Miami-Dade, Orange, Brevard.
  • Signal watch alerts (Coming Soon / Beta intake) — Property Intelligence form captures zoning / permits / tax-calendar / cert watch prefs via Netlify Forms; automated same-day email delivery is not live yet.
