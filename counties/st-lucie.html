Surplus funds (post-sale claim)

When a tax-deed sale closes above the opening bid, Florida Statute §197.582 requires excess proceeds be paid to the prior titleholder or their lienholders. St. Lucie County publishes its surplus workflow at https://www.stlucieclerk.com/tax-deeds/surplus-funds.

How tax-deed sales work in Florida

Florida tax-deed sales follow a uniform statutory framework set by Chapter 197 F.S., but the conduct of the sale (online vendor, bid increment, deposit rules) varies by county. In St. Lucie County, sales are conducted via the county auction platform . Bidders should verify auction-day rules with the Tax Collector's office before participating.

The investor process:

  1. Tax certificate sale (early summer) — investors bid on the right to collect delinquent taxes plus interest.
  2. Two-year hold — certificate accrues interest while the owner has the chance to redeem.
  3. Application for tax deed — the certificate holder applies after two years; a public auction is scheduled.
  4. Tax-deed sale — the property itself is sold to the highest bidder; certificate holder is paid first from proceeds.
  5. Surplus claim (if applicable) — excess proceeds go to lienholders and the former owner under §197.582.
