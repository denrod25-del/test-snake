Florida tax deed surplus funds, county by county

When a tax deed overbids, surplus can sit with the clerk. Missing the claim window costs the former owner. This page maps which counties publish surplus lists and how DeedScout helps you research them.

What this page covers

  • Which counties currently have a surplus portal linked in DeedScout (32 of 67).
  • Where to verify claim procedures (always the official clerk or comptroller).
  • How Pro skip-trace helps locate phones and emails for the recorded prior owner after you confirm a surplus file exists.

Claim timing

Florida Statute Chapter 197 governs excess proceeds after tax deed sales. Deadlines and standing rules differ by county practice. DeedScout does not calculate claim deadlines. Open the county portal and read the clerk instructions before contacting anyone.
