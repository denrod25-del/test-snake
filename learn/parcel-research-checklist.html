Parcel research checklist

  1. Confirm PCN on property appraiser site
  2. Verify address, owner, and assessed value
  3. Check clerk for liens, judgments, and sale status
  4. Review permit history (cached municipal scrapes vs sample labels in DeedScout)
  5. Document findings in parcel notebook (Pro)
  6. Verify all fields on official county source before bidding
