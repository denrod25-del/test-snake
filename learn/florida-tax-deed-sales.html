Florida tax deed sales

Florida counties sell tax deeds when property taxes remain unpaid after statutory notice and certificate processes. Each county uses different auction platforms — RealAuction, LienHub, DeedAuction, or in-person courthouse sales.

Before bidding: Verify sale date, parcel list, opening bid rules, and redemption status on the official clerk and auction sites linked from your county page in DeedScout Tax Deeds.

DeedScout organizes links and research workflow — it is not a substitute for title research, legal review, or official county records.
