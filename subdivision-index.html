How to use this for leads & research. Contractor leads — filter by city, pick subdivisions that are mostly residential (80%+), export CSV, then open parcels for permit history. Investor research — look for high non-homestead % (landlord/rental proxy) combined with your target city. Homestead flags are a rough signal, not verified owner records.
