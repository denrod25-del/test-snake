Plumbing Watch

Tracks every plumbing-related permit across all loaded sources (Plumbing Sub-Permit, Water Heater Changeout, Backflow, Water Filter, Sewer, Repipe, etc.). The hotspot prediction ranks ZIP codes by the ratio of plumbing permits in the last 30 days vs. the prior 30 days — a sustained ratio > 1 suggests aging infrastructure or a developing trend. Not machine learning: it's a transparent recency-weighted growth ranking on the data you've loaded. Ignores active Search-tab filters; this view is always full-population.

Permit data is a public record under Florida's Sunshine Law, sourced from each jurisdiction's permitting portal. This tool searches a locally cached snapshot — not a live query. For authoritative records, visit each jurisdiction's official portal (links in the source health table and on each result row). Permit data is a research signal, not proof of work needed or job availability. No warranty of accuracy — verify any permit before relying on it. Dataset inventory: Data Sources · System Status.
