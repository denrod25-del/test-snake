Surplus Funds Registry

Tax Deed Overbids — Florida

When a tax deed sale produces more than the minimum bid plus statutory fees, the excess proceeds (called surplus funds) are held by the Clerk and may be claimed by the prior owner, lienholders, or junior interest holders under Florida Statute §197.582.
