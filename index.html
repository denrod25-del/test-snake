Florida Public-Records Tools

A small suite of free research tools for Florida real estate, plumbing, and county-records work. Built on live data scraped directly from county and municipal portals — no signup required for any search.

Data is a locally cached snapshot, not a live query. For authoritative records, always confirm with the county's own portal.
