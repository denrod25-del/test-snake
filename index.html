Florida property records are public, but the workflow is broken.

Every county has different clerk websites, property appraiser portals, permit databases, auction platforms, GIS tools, PDFs, and update schedules.

DeedScout brings those scattered records into one research workflow so investors, contractors, title researchers, and real estate professionals can find opportunity signals faster.
