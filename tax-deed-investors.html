Common questions

What is a Florida tax deed sale?

A public auction where the county sells property to recover unpaid taxes after a tax certificate holder applies for a deed. Rules vary by county but follow Chapter 197 F.S.

What should I verify before bidding?

Sale date, parcel list, opening bid, liens, occupancy, zoning, and title issues — always through official county and clerk sources and licensed counsel.

What are surplus funds?

Excess proceeds after a tax deed sale may be claimable by prior owners or lienholders under §197.582. DeedScout links to county surplus portals; Pro unlocks searchable history.
