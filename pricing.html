Frequently asked

Why would people pay if county records are free?

Because the records are free, but the research process is slow, fragmented, and inconsistent. DeedScout saves time by organizing county sources into one workflow.

Can I use DeedScout instead of official county records?

No. DeedScout is a research layer only. Always verify sale dates, parcel details, bid rules, and legal status with the official county source before acting.
